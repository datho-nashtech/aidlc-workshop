# Intent Statement

## Problem Statement

Players cannot see which fields are free and book one without calling the owner, and field owners cannot keep schedules, prices, and bookings in one reliable place. [Q1]

This first version is a simple football field management system. [desc]

## Target Customer

Both players and the field owner feel this pain and use the first version. [Q2] [Q9]

- Players book online and keep a confirmation. [Q1] [Q3]
- The field owner runs the schedule and the prices, can add fields, and can see every booking. [Q1] [Q3]

## Success Metrics

A player can find a free slot and finish a booking, and two people cannot take the same slot. [Q3]

An owner can add fields, set prices, and see every booking. [Q3]

The player also keeps a confirmation of the booking. [Q3]

## Initiative Trigger

An opportunity to take bookings online instead of by phone or in person. [Q4]

## Initial Scope Signal

- Workflow-selected: `classic` (workflow-selected). [scope]
- User-confirmed product boundary for this first version: leave out public hosting, real card payments, admin charts, rescheduling, peak-hour pricing, and recurring schedules. [Q5]

## Assumptions & Open Questions

None.
