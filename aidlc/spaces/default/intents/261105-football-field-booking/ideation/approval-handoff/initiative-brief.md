## Intent and problem

Players cannot see which fields are free and book one without calling the owner, and field owners cannot keep schedules, prices, and bookings in one reliable place. [intent-statement]

This first version is a simple football field management system for one venue with several fields. Both the player and the field owner use it. [intent-statement] [scope-document]

Success: a player can find a free slot and finish a booking; two people cannot take the same slot; the owner can add fields, set prices, and see every booking; the player keeps a confirmation. [intent-statement]

## Scope boundary

**First slice:** owner keeps fields, prices, and the book, and books on behalf of players. [scope-document]

**First-version Must:** fields, prices, schedule, no double booking, owner booking list, player booking, confirmation. Cancel is Should. [intent-backlog]

**Out:** public hosting, real card payments, admin charts, rescheduling, peak-hour pricing, recurring schedules, several venues. [scope-document]

**Order:** fields and prices, then schedule, then a booking that cannot collide, then confirmation. [scope-document]
